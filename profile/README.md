# 👋 First Point

**The company behind [Your Next Tours](https://yournext.tours).**
First Point is a software company in Ankara, Türkiye, founded in 2024. Since May 2026 our team has focused on Your Next Tours. We have also shipped 10 mobile apps on iOS and Android.

## 🎧 Your Next Tours: our focus

Live audio for guided tours, sold to tour guides and tour companies. The guide's phone becomes the broadcast server and the group listens in their own browser after scanning a QR code: no receivers to rent, no app for guests, and no internet needed during the tour.

[Website](https://yournext.tours) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/your-next-tours-audio-guide/id6783743090) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yournext.tours) · [Docs](https://github.com/First-Point/your-next-tours)

Open source from the Your Next Tours team:

- [**your-next-tours-mcp**](https://github.com/First-Point/your-next-tours-mcp): connect AI assistants (Cursor, Claude, VS Code) to Your Next Tours through a hosted MCP server for tour guides
- [**tourist-trip-schema**](https://github.com/First-Point/tourist-trip-schema): build valid schema.org TouristTrip JSON-LD for tour itineraries, with zero dependencies
- [**tour-itinerary**](https://github.com/First-Point/tour-itinerary): a WordPress block for day-by-day tour itineraries with TouristTrip structured data

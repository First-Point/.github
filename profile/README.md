# 👋 Welcome to FirstPoint

**Making things people love to use.**
We build mobile games, apps, AI agents, and blockchain products used worldwide.

## 💡 About Us

**FirstPoint** is a small team of engineers and designers focused on shipping polished products across three domains:

- 📱 **Mobile Games & Apps:** fun, polished mobile games and apps, from word puzzles to everyday utilities
- 🤖 **AI Agents:** autonomous AI agents and marketplaces that make intelligence accessible
- ⛓️ **Blockchain:** decentralized products and protocols pushing the boundaries of Web3
